Privacy Policy

ResQ Operations handles real-time emergency dispatch and telemetry for field units, dispatch centers, and reporting citizens.

Location and Telemetry Data

Location data collected from responder devices is used strictly for active incident routing and triage queueing. Spatial coordinates are processed transiently and cached only for offline navigation tolerance.

Citizen Reports

Emergency alerts submitted through citizen terminals contain incident categories, casualty status, and verified GPS coordinates. This data is transmitted exclusively to authorized dispatch consoles.
